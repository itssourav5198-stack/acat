Assam Cyber Aegis Team

Weaving a safer digital Assam, one thread at a time.

ACAT is an independent, volunteer-run community helping people across Assam recognise scams, stay safe online, and know exactly where to turn when something goes wrong. Guidance and awareness are always free — a hands-on penetration test is free the first time, then a small fee for further support.

Our Mission

Cyber safety for everyone.

Just like a gamosa is woven thread by thread until it holds strong, ACAT is built volunteer by volunteer — ordinary people across Assam sharing what they know so their families, neighbours, and communities don't fall prey to online fraud.

We don't charge for guidance or awareness, we don't ask for your OTP or bank details, and we always point serious cases toward the official cybercrime channels. Every scam victim also gets one free penetration test to check for further compromise — after that, a small ₹579 fee applies to keep the service sustainable.

Every report shared with us is handled with care. For matters involving money or personal documents, we guide you to file directly at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 — our volunteers assist, they don't replace official action.
