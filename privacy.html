Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

ACAT is a volunteer initiative, not a company — but we still take the information you share with us seriously.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Assam Cyber Aegis Team (ACAT) is an independent, volunteer-run community initiative focused on cyber safety awareness across Assam. ACAT is not a registered company or government body, and this policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled.

2. What information we collect

We only collect what you choose to share through our forms:

  • Scam Report form: name, contact number or email, city, scam type, a description of what happened, and an optional screenshot.
  • Volunteer Sign-up form: name, contact number or email, city, and your reason for wanting to join.

We never ask for OTPs, passwords, PINs, card numbers, or banking credentials through this website, and you should never share them with us or anyone else claiming to represent ACAT.

3. How we use your information

Form submissions are sent directly to ACAT's team email so a volunteer can review the report or sign-up and follow up with you. We use this information only to respond to your submission and, where relevant, to help guide you toward official channels such as the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or the 1930 helpline.

4. How your information is stored

Submissions are delivered by email to ACAT's team inbox. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Your choices

You can choose not to fill in optional fields, and you can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request that it be deleted.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, reach out at dhirajvaii97@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
